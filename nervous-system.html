About

This release is dedicated to all my fellow anxiety-ridden Millennials, with each piece reflecting different facets of this experience. It's a sonic journey through a world of mindfulness exercises, chemical imbalances, fear — but also hope, learning new behavioural patterns, and discovering one's full potential.
