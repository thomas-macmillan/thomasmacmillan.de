About

Inspired by the horror novel of the same name by B.R. Yeager, "Negative Space" is an architecturally-themed deep dive into liminal spaces, analog horror, and 90s-era nostalgia. Using only the nylon-strung acoustic guitar, it evokes imaginary places that both comfort and unsettle in equal measure, taking as its cue the ambient "fogcore" music of Akira Yamaoka (composer for the Silent Hill video game franchise). A unique guitar tuning (Eb G D F A E) is used to create harmony that twists and turns - frequently wrongfooting the listener - in combination with rhythms that feel unstable and volatile. Such unpredictability is offset by strong melodies which provide a more optimistic counterweight to the EP's heavy and dramatic tone.
