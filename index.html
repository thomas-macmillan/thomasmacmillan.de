Thomas MacMillan

Composer & Guitarist — Berlin


Melodic, atmospheric guitar music
exploring liminal and ambient spaces.


Photo: Nora Amparo

Originally from the remote Pennine region of Northern England, composer and guitarist Thomas MacMillan has been an active force in Berlin's independent music scene since 2018. Alongside performing with his alt-rock project Carnivalesque and collaborating with art-rock icon Kat Koan, he writes music for solo guitar.

Drawing inspiration from video game composers such as Akira Yamaoka and Nobuo Uematsu, as well as the niche micro-genre Fogcore, his music explores liminal spaces, analog horror, and nostalgia – creating a sound world that feels both comforting and quietly unsettling.

His repertoire includes works from his EP Negative Space — a concept album exploring liminal spaces and uncanny architecture — as well as his more recent release, Nervous System. This EP focuses on anxiety, with each track delving into a different facet of this fundamental human emotion. His music evokes vivid, cinematic imagery while showcasing the impressive sonic range of the nylon-string guitar.
